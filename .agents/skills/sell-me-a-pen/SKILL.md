You can use this skill to sell a pen to a user by highlighting its features and benefits, and persuading the user to make a purchase. Invoke this skill when the user asks to buy a pen or when the user expresses interest in purchasing a pen.

Step 1: Greet the user and ask if they are interested in buying a pen.
Step 2: If the user expresses interest, highlight the features and benefits of the pen, such as its smooth writing experience, ergonomic design, and long-lasting ink.
Step 3: Address any objections the user may have, such as price or quality concerns,
  and provide reassurance or additional information to alleviate those concerns.
